# Student guide

## Signing in

Use the account your institution issued. If you have turned on two step sign in,
you will be asked for a code from your phone after your password. Lost the
phone? Any of your recovery codes works instead, once.

## Your dashboard

What is coming up, and what has been announced. Start here.

## Your courses

**Courses** shows what you are registered for. Open one and you see the
sections and lessons your lecturer has published, with your progress across the
top.

- A lesson with a date next to it opens then.
- **Mark as done** when you have finished a lesson. Progress counts the required
  lessons only, so optional reading does not make you look behind.
- The platform counts your time only while the tab is actually open.

## Assessments

**Assessments** lists everything set for you, with its state: not started, due
soon, submitted, or result available.

**Quizzes and tests.** Start when you are ready. A time limit runs from the
moment you start, the clock is on the screen, and your answers save every few
seconds. If the time runs out the paper is submitted rather than lost. Once you
submit you cannot change your answers.

**Assignments.** Upload your file, replace it as often as you like, and submit
when you are ready. After you submit, ask your lecturer if you need it reopened.

**Late work.** If your assessment accepts late submissions, you can still submit
after the due date and a penalty is applied. If it does not, the due date is the
end of it. The screen tells you which before you start.

**Your marks** appear when your lecturer releases results for the whole class,
not one at a time.

## Your record

**Academic records** shows your results by term, your credits, and any
certificates issued to you. Marks appear here once released.

Each certificate carries a verification code. Anyone you give it to can check it
at your institution's verify page. They see what you were awarded, by whom and
when, and nothing else about you.

## Your account

**Your account** shows what you owe, what you have paid, and your receipts.

To send proof of payment: upload the slip, enter the amount, the date and the
reference you used at your bank. It goes to the finance office for checking, and
your account is credited once they have looked at it. The status tells you where
it is.

## Attendance

Where your lecturer uses self check-in, they read out a code at the start of the
session and you enter it. It works from shortly before the session until part
way through. If you miss that window, speak to your lecturer.

## Messages and discussions

Ask questions the class would benefit from in the course discussion. Use
messages for anything private: your lecturers and the administration are there.

## Getting help

**Support** raises a ticket. Say what you were trying to do and what happened;
you do not need to work out why.

If something about the platform itself stops you working, the accessibility page
says what we know about and how to tell us the rest.
